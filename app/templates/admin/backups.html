Restoring, and changing what is captured

Both are host actions. Restoring overwrites a live database and can overlay gigabytes of files, so it asks for confirmation at a terminal rather than behind a button here.

Restore one backup:

sudo ./deploy/scripts/restore-instance.sh <instance> /srv/tinymrp/backups/<instance>/<backup>

Start capturing deliverables, onto another drive:

sudo ./deploy/scripts/backup-all.sh --deliverables-dest /mnt/backupdrive/tinymrp --deliverables-frequency monthly

Full reference: docs/deployment/10-operations.md.
