# What TinyMRP is, and what it is not

Decided by the owner on 2026-08-07. This document exists so that marketing
copy, sales conversations and the help all describe the same product.

Anyone writing about TinyMRP should be able to check a claim against this page
in under a minute. If a claim is not supported here, it does not go out.

## In one sentence

TinyMRP moves engineering information to purchasing and to the workshop, for
small engineering offices.

## The honest framing of the name

The product is called TinyMRP, and it is **not a complete MRP**. That is not a
gap to apologise for — it is the scope. But the name will do the promising if
nobody else does, so wherever the name appears in a commercial context, the
scope belongs beside it.

Say what it does. Do not say "MRP" and let the reader fill in the rest.

## What it does

- **Parts and revisions** — a controlled record per part revision, with the
  attributes SolidWorks writes.
- **BOMs** — multi-level structures, where-used, and flattened views.
- **Engineering files** — drawings, models, PDFs and derived formats, kept
  against the part revision they belong to.
- **Drawing review and markup** — comment threads anchored to a drawing.
- **Controlled document packs** — a compiled, indexed, stamped PDF bundle for
  a job or an order.
- **Jobs and purchase listings** — what to buy and what to make, in a form
  purchasing and the workshop can act on.
- **SolidWorks integration** — an add-in that writes properties and uploads
  packages directly from CAD.

## What it does not do

None of the following exists, and none may be claimed, implied, or shown on a
roadmap slide as though it were near:

- Inventory of any kind. No stock levels, no available-to-promise.
- Warehouses, bins, or transfers.
- Purchase receipts or material issues.
- MRP explosion or demand netting.
- Lead times, safety stock, or reorder policies.
- Routings, work centres, or capacity planning.
- Costing or variance analysis.
- Lot or serial traceability.
- Cycle counting or stock adjustments.
- Planning exception messages.
- Accounting integration.

If a prospect needs stock control, TinyMRP is the wrong product and saying so
early is cheaper for everyone than saying so during an implementation.

## Who it is for

Small engineering offices that design in SolidWorks and need the output of
that work to reach purchasing and the shop floor without being retyped.

The value is that the BOM, the files and the revisions that purchasing and the
workshop receive are the ones engineering actually released — not a
spreadsheet someone exported last week.

## Who it is not for

- Anyone who needs to know how many of something they have.
- Manufacturers running production planning off stock and capacity.
- Organisations that need TinyMRP to be their system of record for cost.
